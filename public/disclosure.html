FTC & Commercial Affiliate Disclosure

Last Updated: January 2026

Editorial Disclosure: When you buy through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission at no additional cost to you.

1. Compliance with FTC Guidelines

In accordance with the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines regarding the use of endorsements and testimonials in advertising, KnowledgeShare maintains full transparency regarding our commercial relationships, affiliate partnerships, and revenue streams.

2. What Are Affiliate Links?

Many of the outbound links embedded in our product reviews, hardware comparisons, and buyer guides are "affiliate links." When you click on an affiliate link (such as "Check Latest Price" or "View on Store") and complete a transaction on a merchant website (e.g., Amazon, Razer, Lenovo, Apple, or retailer stores), KnowledgeShare receives a small referral commission directly from the vendor.

Important: Clicking an affiliate link does NOT increase the price you pay. Merchants pay our commission directly out of their marketing budget.

3. Editorial Independence & Product Testing

Our editorial integrity is our highest priority. Commercial relationships do not dictate or influence our editorial evaluations, star ratings, pros & cons, or benchmark conclusions. Our recommendations are based on rigorous empirical testing, hardware analysis, real-world ergonomics, and thermal stress testing.

4. Display Advertising

KnowledgeShare displays programmatic advertisements served by third-party networks (such as Google AdSense). Display ad units are labeled clearly with "Sponsored" or "Advertisement" headers to differentiate commercial ads from our independent editorial content.

5. Questions & Feedback

If you have any questions regarding our affiliate relationships or review methodology, please reach out to our editorial team at editor@knowledgeshare.com.
