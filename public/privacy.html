Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At KnowledgeShare, accessible from our digital platform, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by KnowledgeShare and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for an account, publish articles, or interact with features on our site, we may collect personal information including:

  • Account Credentials (username, email address, password hashes).
  • User Preferences (saved streams, theme preferences).
  • Log Data (IP addresses, browser types, timestamp logs, and referring URLs).

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

KnowledgeShare uses 'cookies' and standard web storage mechanisms to store information including visitors' preferences, authentication tokens (JWTs), and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and preferences.

3. Third-Party Advertising & AdSense

Third-party ad servers or ad networks (such as Google AdSense) use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on KnowledgeShare. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that KnowledgeShare has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

4. Affiliate Links & Analytics

Our website contains commercial affiliate links. When you click on an affiliate link and make a purchase, third-party affiliate networks (including Amazon Associates, merchant networks, and partner programs) may set cookies to track sales attribution. These cookies contain no personally identifiable information.

5. Data Protection Rights

We would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict processing of their personal data. If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you.

6. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at privacy@knowledgeshare.com.
